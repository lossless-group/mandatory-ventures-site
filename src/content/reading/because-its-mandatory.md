**man•da•to•ry** *(adj.)*: so important it is required.

A **mandate** is what large-scale capital calls a strategic decision to get
exposure to something. A family office, an institution, a sovereign or
strategic fund decides it needs a position in the companies that went from
startup to scaleup.

Deciding is the easy part. The fastest-growing companies fill their rounds
with insiders and early backers, and the people on those cap tables are the
ones who saw it first and bet the real bet.

Mandatory exists to match the two: **mandates to earned access.**

Because it's mandatory.
